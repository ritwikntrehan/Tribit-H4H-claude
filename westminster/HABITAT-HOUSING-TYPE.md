# Recommended Habitat OC Westminster prototype: six attached ownership townhomes

**Recommendation, not a confirmed project:** A six-unit attached townhouse/rowhouse project sold as deed-restricted affordable homeownership is a credible representative case for Habitat for Humanity of Orange County (California). Habitat OC reports six townhomes under construction in Santa Ana in 2026; its previous developments include attached duplexes in Fullerton and Stanton, plus detached single-family homes. Habitat has not been verified as planning this exact project in Westminster.

**Primary source:** https://habitatoc.org/leaders-build-2026-raises-150000-for-affordable-homeownership-orange-county/ ; https://habitatoc.org/our-homes/

## Why this typology

A six-unit project captures Habitat's repeatable small-multifamily ownership model and creates a substantive planning/entitlement packet. Detached one-off houses are a valid second template, but may use zoning clearance rather than Development Review.

**Important zoning correction:** Westminster Municipal Code 17.520.010(A)(2) says **new residential projects with three or more units** require Development Review; the city's ADU webpage says four or more. The code and webpage conflict. For six units both thresholds are met; for three units confirm with Planning. Source: https://ecode360.com/43536828 and https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/community-development/planning-division/accessory-dwelling-units

**Do not infer entitlement from unit count.** Verify parcel zoning, density, ownership configuration, subdivision/condominium map needs, access, parking, site constraints, affordable housing incentives, CEQA/streamlining and whether any alternative ministerial process is available. Westminster updated its mixed-use zoning effective June 12, 2026: https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/community-development/planning-division/advanced-planning/zoning-gp-update

## Core documents for the townhome planning packet

- Development Review Application PL-101 and detailed project narrative
- Applicant, owner and agent contact information, owner authorization/signature
- Grant deed or ownership verification; current title report, easements and legal description
- Existing conditions/ALTA or topographic survey, site plan, proposed unit layouts/floor plans, elevations, roof plan, sections, grading and landscaping drawings as applicable
- Residential energy questionnaire, hazardous waste statement, colored elevations, materials/colors board
- Water Quality Management Plan affidavit and WQMP as determined
- Replacement housing determination and potential SB 330 tenant/replacement-unit information
- Park impact fee acknowledgment; current fees
- Conditional fire authority, public notification, environmental, density bonus, subdivision/condominium, engineering and technical materials

See [SUBMITTAL-CHECKLIST.md](SUBMITTAL-CHECKLIST.md) and [official PL-101 packet](https://www.westminster-ca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/536/637544150393130000). These documents must be checked against the city's current forms and the real project facts.

## Alternative cases

- **Single detached ownership home:** Habitat OC also builds this. Easier packet, but less useful as a representative entitlement workflow.
- **Duplex or paired homes:** historically demonstrated by Habitat OC; may have different zoning clearance route.
- **Condominium conversion:** Habitat OC also undertakes this, but acquisition/conversion/tenant obligations are a distinct workflow and should not be folded into the new-construction MVP.
