# Development Review packet: document checklist

Derived from the [official Westminster Development Review Application Packet (PL-101)](https://www.westminster-ca.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/536/637544150393130000), checklist pages 2–4. **City staff must confirm applicability and current instructions.** For a four-unit new residential development:

## Packet states generally required for this type of project

- [ ] Completed Development Review application, project/property/APN and applicant/owner/agent details; **property owner signs authorization** (packet p.1).
- [ ] Detailed project description and requested entitlement(s).
- [ ] Housing Questionnaire: Energy Efficiency & Conservation (packet p.5; new residential development).
- [ ] Hazardous Waste and Substances Statement (packet p.6; development).
- [ ] Ownership verification, e.g. recent deed, title report or closing statement; additional signatures/entity/trust documents as applicable.
- [ ] Full title report dated within the preceding six months, including exhibits, easements and CC&Rs (development).
- [ ] Planning-stage plans/maps as directed by staff. Packet lists ALTA survey, site plan, floor plans, elevations, roof plans, sections, landscape and grading plans as possible components (pp.9–12).
- [ ] Colored building elevations (new development).
- [ ] Materials and colors board (new development; packet says no larger than 8 × 14 inches).
- [ ] Water Quality Management Plan affidavit (new development or significant redevelopment; packet p.14).
- [ ] Replacement Housing Determination Form (new residential development in residential or mixed-use zone; pp.26–29).
- [ ] Park impact fee acknowledgment for net new residential units, subject to exemptions (pp.20–25).
- [ ] Digital copies of documents, plans and maps in the format now required by Planning Division.
- [ ] Current application processing fees, confirmed with Planning Division.

## Conditional / ask staff

- [ ] OCFA planning and development service request and associated fee, **if OCFA review is required**. Packet notes the request form may need to be obtained at City Hall; its printed fee may be outdated.
- [ ] Conceptual or non-priority Water Quality Management Plan, as determined by engineering.
- [ ] Public notification 500-foot radius map, property-owner/occupant mailing list and affidavit, if notification required (pp.15–19).
- [ ] Three-dimensional colored renderings, if required.
- [ ] Photo simulations (packet labels optional).
- [ ] Extra owner, trustee, corporate, HOA or joint-owner documentation, depending on title.
- [ ] General Land Use and Development application if multiple entitlements, as directed by staff.
- [ ] Density Bonus Application if claiming density bonus; determine supporting eligibility and affordability documentation.
- [ ] SB 330 replacement housing documentation and any additional tenant/replacement-unit forms if applicable.
- [ ] Environmental studies, public agency reviews, subdivision maps, grading/engineering documents, and additional permits as dictated by site and entitlement.

## Later stage, not part of initial planning packet

Building permit application, architectural construction drawings, structural plans/calculations, Title 24 energy calculations, MEP plans, and other Building Division plan-check documentation. See [Building Division requirements](https://www.westminster-ca.gov/departments/community-development/building-division/building-permits-plan-checks).

## Warnings

The city's PL-101 PDF is an older version. It includes fee examples, physical plan counts, and CD/flash-drive instructions; **do not assume those are current**. The city currently advertises Westminster Build. No real parcel, owner, APN, or technical plans have been supplied. This is an application inventory, not a completed submission.
