# Human review checklist

- [ ] Confirm current city application type, instructions, and forms.
- [ ] Verify applicant, owner, property, parcel and project facts against original documents.
- [ ] Confirm every required attachment and applicable study.
- [ ] Resolve all missing, contradictory, or unverified information.
- [ ] Confirm fees and code references against current official sources.
- [ ] Review consistency of forms, drawings and supporting narratives.
- [ ] Obtain required human signatures and approvals.
- [ ] Submit through the city's official process; the assistant does not submit.
